Sourcing Milestones

Our Sourcing and Mobilization Workflow

We manage administrative, testing, and travel logistics to ensure smooth talent delivery.

01

Demand & Job Specification

Provide details on vacancies, job descriptions, technical certifications, salary parameters, and local host-nation contract constraints.

02

Sourcing & Screening

We source matching profiles from our digital database and target networks, front-loading resume reviews and background verification checks.

03

Technical Vetting & Selection

Organize virtual panels or physical trade tests at our certified facilities, ensuring only validated candidates are selected.

04

Medical & Compliance Checks

Coordinate standard GAMCA medical checkups, gather local Police Clearance Certificates (PCC), and complete eMigrate validation parameters.

05

Visa & Travel Coordination

Submit completed files to the relevant embassies for visa endorsement and book flight travel logistics once approved.

06

Pre-Departure Orientation

Conduct final orientations (PDOS) focusing on site safety (HSE), local host-country labor guidelines, cultural orientation, and company values.
