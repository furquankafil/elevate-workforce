Immigration Compliance

Preparing for Overseas Placement

Selected candidates must complete standard medical and documentation verifications.

GAMCA Medical Check

Required for GCC placements (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar). Verifies physical fitness and health clearance.

PCC & Apostille

Required for European Union work permits. Involves gathering local Police Clearance Certificates and verified degrees.

Pre-Departure Briefings

Orientation seminars covering workplace safety (HSE), local laws, contract details, and cultural guidelines.
